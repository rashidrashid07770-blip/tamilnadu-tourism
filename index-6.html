About

Tamil Nadu is a state in the southeastern part of India, with the Bay of Bengal to the east and the Indian Ocean at its southern tip. Its capital and largest city is Chennai. The name means the Land of the Tamils, and the state is the home of Tamil, one of the oldest living classical languages in the world. It is divided into 38 districts, each with its own landmarks, food and traditions. The coastline stretches about 1,076 km.

Tamil Nadu is best known as the land of temples. More than a thousand historic temples stand here, from the Chola masterpieces of Thanjavur, Gangaikonda Cholapuram and Darasuram to the Meenakshi Temple of Madurai. The Pallava monuments of Mahabalipuram and the Great Living Chola Temples are UNESCO World Heritage Sites. Dravidian architecture, with its towering gopurams, carved pillars and sacred tanks, reached its peak here. Classical arts such as Bharatanatyam and Carnatic music grew in these temple towns.

The land is as varied as its culture. The Nilgiri and Anaimalai hills offer tea estates, hill stations and wildlife, the Kaveri delta feeds the state as its rice bowl, and long beaches line the coast. Tamil cuisine adds to the experience, from soft idli and crisp dosa to Chettinad curries, Madurai parotta and a banana-leaf meal. Festivals like Pongal, Karthigai Deepam and Jallikattu show the living traditions of the people. Visitors are welcomed with warm hospitality in every district. This guide brings together the places, districts and flavours to help you plan your journey.
